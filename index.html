Application Overview

DC-Cranes is a specialized document scanner and operations documentation utility built for heavy machinery logistics, site inspections, and equipment rentals. The application is built on an ephemeral, client-side zero-retention model that syncs paperwork directly to your Google Drive.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 | Version 1.0

DC-Cranes ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This policy details how our Android application handles device hardware access, identity authentication, and document cloud synchronization.

1. Permissions & Data Access

  • Google Account Identity: When authenticating via Google Sign-In, we access your basic account credentials (name and email address) solely to identify authorized site operators and verify app sessions.
  • Google Drive Storage (drive.file Scope): DC-Cranes accesses your Google Drive solely through the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope. This permission is strictly restricted to creating, uploading, and reading files or folders generated directly by the DC-Cranes application. We cannot access, view, modify, or delete any other files, folders, or photos in your personal Google Drive.
  • Camera Hardware: Camera access is utilized exclusively for real-time document capture (invoices, work orders, machine inspection sheets). Images are processed ephemerally on your local device.

2. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

DC-Cranes adheres strictly to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • Google user data and Drive tokens are used solely to provide user-facing document capture and cloud synchronization capabilities.
  • We do not transfer or disclose Google user data to external third parties, marketing networks, or data brokers.
  • We do not use Google user data or document contents to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
  • We do not retain copies of your documents on external corporate servers. Files reside exclusively in your personal Google Drive account.

3. Ephemeral Storage & Data Retention

DC-Cranes employs a strict zero-retention architecture:

  • During scanning, image frames and compiled PDFs are written temporarily to volatile application cache memory (context.cacheDir).
  • Immediately upon successful synchronization with your personal Google Drive or export through the Android system share sheet, local temporary files are permanently purged from the device.
  • No operational databases or document backups are maintained on intermediary servers.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data transmission occurs strictly between your device and Google's authenticated API endpoints using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3) encryption.

5. Security & Revocation

Your authentication tokens are stored locally using Android system security mechanisms. You can review, modify, or revoke DC-Cranes' access to your Google account at any time via your Google Account Permissions Manager.
