Application Overview

DC-Cranes Vault is a secure mobile document capture, inspection management, and cloud storage utility developed for industrial equipment operations, crane logistics, and job-site record keeping. The application employs an ephemeral zero-retention model that synchronizes documents directly to the user's personal Google Drive without intermediary servers.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 | Version 1.0

DC-Cranes Vault ("we," "our," or "us"), developed by CodeSoul Labs, is committed to safeguarding user data. This policy details how our Android application handles authentication, device hardware access, and cloud document synchronization.

1. Permissions & Data Access

  • Google Account Identity: When authenticating via Google Sign-In, we access your basic profile identity (name and email address) strictly to authenticate the operator session.
  • Google Drive Storage (drive.file Scope): DC-Cranes Vault accesses your Google Drive solely through the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope. This access is strictly limited to files and folders created or opened directly by DC-Cranes Vault. The application has zero capability to read, view, modify, or delete any other personal files, folders, or images in your Google Drive.
  • Camera Hardware: Utilized solely for real-time document capture (delivery receipts, inspection sheets, and machine maintenance paperwork). Images are processed temporarily in volatile memory.

2. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

DC-Cranes Vault adheres strictly to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • Google user data is used solely to provide user-facing document scanning and personal Drive synchronization.
  • We do not transfer, sell, or disclose Google user data to third parties, advertising platforms, or data brokers.
  • We do not use Google user data or document contents to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
  • No user documents are hosted or preserved on external servers; files reside exclusively within the user's personal Google Drive account.

3. Ephemeral Architecture & Data Retention

DC-Cranes Vault operates on a strict zero-retention architecture:

  • Scanned camera frames and generated multi-page PDFs reside temporarily within internal application cache (context.cacheDir).
  • Upon successful upload to your Google Drive or sharing through the system share sheet, local temporary files are immediately purged from the device.
  • We do not operate backend user databases or document retention relays.

4. Data Security & Revocation

All network traffic is encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3). You can inspect or revoke DC-Cranes Vault's authorization at any time via your Google Account Security Settings.
